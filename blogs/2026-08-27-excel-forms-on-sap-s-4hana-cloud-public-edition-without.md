If you've worked with SAP on-premise, you probably know XLSX Workbench — Igor Borodin's brilliant tool where business users design Excel forms visually and ABAP just supplies the data. There's one problem: it's built on SAP GUI, OLE and the Web Repository. None of that exists on S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition.
