Distributed Energy Resources are rapidly moving from the grid edge to the center of utility transformation. As solar, batteries, EV chargers, flexible loads, and prosumers become active participants in the energy ecosystem, utilities must rethink how they manage grid operations, customer engagement, and enterprise processes. This article explores why DER is no longer a peripheral technology initiative, but a core operating model for the future utility.
