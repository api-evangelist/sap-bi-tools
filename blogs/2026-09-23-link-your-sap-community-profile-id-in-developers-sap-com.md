The blog/page explains how to connect your SAP Community Profile to your developers.sap.com account so your community identity is visible across the SAP tutorial platform Step 1 : Go to https://developers.sap.com and Click on My Completions Step 2 : Go to your SAP Community page and Click on your Community Profile and copy the Profile ID Step 3 : Enter your SAP Community Profile ID from previous Step Step 4 : Link is completed successfully
